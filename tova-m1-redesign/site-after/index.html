Safe and private by design

Made for homes with kids, pets and privacy.

Tova lives in your home, so it's built to be gentle around people and to keep what it sees to itself.

  • Gentle by design

    Its arms are torque-limited and stop the moment they touch something unexpected. Top speed is 0.6 m/s, slower than walking pace. Built to the UL 3300 safety standard for home robots.

  • What it sees stays home

    Maps and camera images are processed on the robot itself. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share a recording to teach a new task.

  • You always know when it's looking

    A light on Tova's head is on whenever a camera is in use. Slide the shutter to cover the head camera, or say "Tova, stop listening" to turn off the microphone.

  • Delete everything, anytime

    Erase your maps, recordings and account in one step from the Tova app. We never sell your data.
