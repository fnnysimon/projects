## UX Copy: Tova M1, trust and reassurance blocks

**Context:**
- **Where:** three new blocks on the landing page, from the P1/P2 insights in `04-ux-discovery.md`.
- **User state:** Maya (the parent short on time) is interested but sceptical. She's doing a trust check (stage 4) and a cost check (stage 5) before putting down $99.
- **Tone:** warm, plain and reassuring, following `brand.md`: no jargon, no ALL CAPS, sentence case.
- **Constraints:** short enough to scan on a phone. Each item has a title of 5 words or fewer and a body of 2 sentences or fewer.

> ⚠️ **Tova is fictional, so these product claims are placeholders.** Before anything ships, product and legal must confirm every claim marked 🔶 in the sign-off list at the end. Copy that promises safety, privacy, warranties or financing creates real obligations.

---

### 1. "Safe and private by design" (new section, between the comparison table and the FAQ)

### Recommended Copy
- **Eyebrow:** Safe and private by design
- **Heading:** Made for homes with kids, pets and privacy.
- **Intro:** Tova lives in your home, so it's built to be gentle around people and to keep what it sees to itself.

| Title | Body |
|---|---|
| **Gentle by design** | Its arms are torque-limited and stop the moment they touch something unexpected. Top speed is 0.6 m/s, slower than walking pace. Built to the UL 3300 safety standard for home robots. 🔶 |
| **What it sees stays home** | Maps and camera images are processed on the robot itself. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share a recording to teach a new task. 🔶 |
| **You always know when it's looking** | A light on Tova's head is on whenever a camera is in use. Slide the shutter to cover the head camera, or say "Tova, stop listening" to turn off the microphone. 🔶 |
| **Delete everything, anytime** | Erase your maps, recordings and account in one step from the Tova app. We never sell your data. 🔶 |

### Alternatives (heading)
| Option | Copy | Tone | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| A (chosen) | Made for homes with kids, pets and privacy. | Warm, concrete | Parents: names the three things they worry about |
| B | Gentle with your family. Quiet about your home. | Lyrical, memorable | Brand-led campaigns, if research shows privacy is the main worry |
| C | Safe around people. Private by default. | Direct, factual | Comparison-minded buyers, press kit |

### Rationale
- **It answers both stage 4 questions** ("is it safe with the kids?" and "is it watching us?") in the order parents ask them: safety first, then privacy.
- **Each title is a benefit; each body is the proof.** Concrete numbers (0.6 m/s), named mechanisms (light, shutter, voice command) and a named standard replace a vague "we take privacy seriously".
- **"We never sell your data"** is the one line people look for. It sits last, so the block ends on a firm promise.
- **"Unless you choose to share a recording"** is honest about the one case where data leaves the home: teaching skills, which the marketplace depends on. Hiding it would backfire.

### 2. "Included with every Tova M1" (order band, under the price)

### Recommended Copy
- **Title:** Included with every Tova M1
- **List:**
  - All 6 everyday tasks shown above, ready on day one
  - Free software updates, including new built-in tasks 🔶
  - The Tova Studio app, to teach it tasks of your own
  - Charging dock and a 2.5-hour fast charger
- **Note:** Optional: extra skills from the marketplace. Many are free, some creators charge a small fee. You never need a paid skill for the tasks above. 🔶

### Alternatives (note on paid skills)
| Option | Copy | Tone | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| A (chosen) | Optional: extra skills from the marketplace. Many are free, some creators charge a small fee. You never need a paid skill for the tasks above. | Reassuring, complete | Removes the "hidden subscription" fear head-on |
| B | No subscription required. Extra skills from creators are optional. | Short, punchy | A badge next to the price, if space is tight |
| C | Everything above works out of the box. Marketplace skills are extras, priced by their creators. | Neutral | A FAQ answer or the specs page |

### Rationale
- **It answers the stage 5 question** "are there subscriptions?" (insight 3) right where the price is, instead of leaving people to infer it from "$1.99/mo" in the marketplace.
- **"Ready on day one"** ties the list back to the six tasks above, so the promise and the proof are linked.
- **"You never need a paid skill for the tasks above"** is the key sentence: an explicit "no", not "most features are free".
- **The charger time (2.5 h)** reuses the figure already in the battery tooltip, so the facts stay consistent.

### 3. Peace-of-mind row (order band, full width under the form)

### Recommended Copy
| Title | Body |
|---|---|
| **30-day home trial** | Not the right fit? Send it back within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. We cover return shipping. 🔶 |
| **2-year warranty** | Parts and labor are covered. If something breaks, we pick it up and repair it for free. 🔶 |
| **Free delivery and setup** | Free delivery anywhere in the US. A Tova specialist joins a video call to help you set up. 🔶 |
| **Pay over time** | Pay the $1,591 balance in full when it ships, or in 12 monthly payments. Subject to approval. 🔶 |

### Alternatives (trial title)
| Option | Copy | Tone | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| A (chosen) | 30-day home trial | Inviting | Frames the return policy as "try it", not "return it" |
| B | 30-day returns | Plain, factual | If legal prefers neutral wording |
| C | Try it at home for 30 days | Warm, longer | Hero badge or ad copy |

### Rationale
- **It gives risk-averse buyers a safety net** beyond the deposit refund (insight 4). That matters most for a $1,690 purchase they can't try in a store.
- **Ordered by the buyer's fear:** "what if I don't like it" (trial), then "what if it breaks" (warranty), "how does it get here" (delivery), "can I afford it" (financing).
- **"Not the right fit?"** acknowledges the doubt without making the product sound fragile.
- **"Subject to approval"** is the minimum honest qualifier for financing. It keeps the copy short without implying guaranteed credit or a 0% rate, which would need full terms.
- **"We pick it up"** removes the hassle people imagine when a large device breaks.

### Consistency check (same terms everywhere)
- **Deposit:** "$99 refundable deposit", "$1,591 balance", "Q1 2027". These match the hero, mid-page call to action, order note, FAQ and confirmation modal.
- **"Tasks" vs "skills":**
  - **Tasks** = what Tova does out of the box (the 6 shown).
  - **Skills** = marketplace add-ons.

  The new copy keeps that split. The marketplace intro still says "your robot learns the task", which is acceptable.
- **"Tova app" vs "Tova Studio app":** the privacy block says "the Tova app" for deleting data, and "Included" says "the Tova Studio app" for teaching. 🔶 Confirm whether these are one app or two, then use one name everywhere.

### Localization Notes
- **US-specific content:** "UL 3300", "ZIP", "anywhere in the US" and monthly financing would all need market-specific versions (for example CE marking and different consumer law in the EU, where 14-day withdrawal is a legal right, not a perk).
- **Speed:** "0.6 m/s, slower than walking pace" works in metric markets. For US readers, consider adding "(about 1.3 mph)".
- **Text expansion:** "Tova, stop listening" is a voice command. Translate it only if the robot understands it in that language, otherwise keep it in English, in quotes.
- **"Not the right fit?"** is idiomatic. Translate the meaning ("Changed your mind?"), not the words.
- **Length:** German and French run 20–35% longer. The 4-column rows wrap to 2 columns below 900px and 1 column below 480px, so they can absorb it.

### Sign-off list (claims to confirm before launch) 🔶
1. Compliance with UL 3300, and certification status.
2. On-device processing of maps and images, and exactly what is uploaded and when.
3. Camera indicator light, head-camera shutter, and the "stop listening" voice command.
4. One-step deletion of maps, recordings and account, and "we never sell your data" (must match the privacy policy).
5. Free software updates and new built-in tasks: for how long?
6. "You never need a paid skill for the tasks above."
7. 30-day home trial with free return shipping.
8. 2-year warranty, including free pickup and repair.
9. Free US delivery (including Alaska and Hawaii?), and the video setup call.
10. Financing: provider, term (12 months), APR and the legal disclosure.
11. One app or two ("Tova app" / "Tova Studio app").
